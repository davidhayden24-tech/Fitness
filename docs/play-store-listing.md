## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
AdaptFit is the home workout app that actually listens when something hurts.

Every other bodyweight workout app hands you the same fixed routine no
matter what. Tell them your knee hurts and you get a "skip for today"
button - tomorrow, the same exercise is right back in your plan.

AdaptFit is different. Flag a sore knee, a bad shoulder, or just give an
exercise a thumbs-down, and AdaptFit permanently reroutes around it -
substituting a biomechanically equivalent movement that still works the
same muscles, in every future session, not just today.

WHAT MAKES ADAPTFIT DIFFERENT

- Persistent adaptive rerouting: flag pain once, and AdaptFit swaps that
  exercise out of your plan for good - not just for today's session.
- Injury-safe mode: tell AdaptFit about a knee, lower back, shoulder, or
  wrist issue, and it automatically excludes and replaces contraindicated
  movements across your entire weekly plan.
- A coach that checks in: before each session, AdaptFit asks how you're
  feeling and adjusts on the spot - short on time, sore, or just not up
  for legs today, tell it and your session changes to match.
- Zero equipment, zero excuses: every exercise uses just your bodyweight -
  no gym, no gear, no membership.
- See exactly how it's adapted for you: your progress screen shows a real
  audit trail of every substitution AdaptFit has made on your behalf.

WHO IT'S FOR

Beginners and returning exercisers who want a "press play and go"
home workout routine without spreadsheets, without a personal trainer,
and without getting hurt by a program that doesn't know anything about
your body.

FAIR PRICING

Plan generation and adaptive rerouting are free - no paywall on the core
feature that makes AdaptFit different. The conversational check-in coach
is part of AdaptFit Plus, our optional subscription.

AdaptFit is not a medical device and doesn't provide medical advice.
Always consult a healthcare provider before starting a new exercise
program, especially with an existing injury.
```
